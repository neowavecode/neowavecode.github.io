Frequently Asked Questions

How do I use the app?

Simply place your phone in a VR headset (like Cardboard), open the app, and look at your hands or limbs. The app mirrors the camera feed to create a visual symmetry effect.

Does it record my camera?

No. All image processing happens locally on your device in real time. No video is ever recorded or uploaded to any server.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

VR Mirror is completely free and needs no sign-up or account. It is supported by discreet ads.

What is the tracking module?

An optional tool for professionals to manage users, log sessions, review progress radars and export PDF reports — all stored locally on the device.
